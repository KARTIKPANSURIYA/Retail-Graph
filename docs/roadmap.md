# Roadmap

## M0 — foundation (this repository)

Typed contracts, synthetic fixtures, integrity checks, transparent smoke metrics, CI, and explicit
unknowns. Exit: all local quality commands pass without private data or downloads.

## M1 — verified ingestion and reference reproduction

Accept/review terms; pin dataset revisions; document exact schemas, published splits, and official
protocol; add strict converters and metadata-only golden samples; reproduce available baselines.
Exit: independently auditable commands and expected ranges, without claiming new performance.

## M2 — research models

Run the planned view/context/pose/time/imbalance ablations and uncertainty analysis. Evaluate
subject/session/store generalization with group confidence intervals and error slices. Exit:
reproducible evidence with limitations, privacy review, and model/data cards.

## M3 — controlled field pilot

After governance approval, validate anonymous short-lived tracks, shelf maps, drift, latency,
retention, estimated-attention language, and opt-out processes in representative stores. POS joins
or interventions require separate consent, security, and causal protocols.

## M4 — product decision

Only validated requirements justify hardened RTSP ingest, local serving, event storage, aggregate
APIs, monitoring, POS integration, and dashboard work. Assess licensing/commercial rights anew.
No facial recognition; no SKU-attention, gaze, placement, or conversion claims beyond evidence.
